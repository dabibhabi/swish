### Exit 13/14: Queens-Midtown Tunnel portal and toll plaza
- Lanes each way / HOV lane: tunnel has 4 lanes in two tubes (2 per tube) [QMT]. Westbound HOV-3 lane in the morning, separated by a movable concrete barrier, on the 2-mile approach [NYCR].
- Surface: asphalt approach (photo `exit-13-14/`). Tunnel walls: white tile with gold and blue stripes (re-tiled 2017-18) [QMT].
- Median: concrete Jersey barrier on the viaduct (photo `exit-13-14/Long_Island_Expwy_td_2022-04-13_036.jpg`).
- Outer edge: concrete parapet on the viaduct; the road descends from a viaduct into the tunnel [QMT].
- Overhead signs: shield "WEST 495" on the approach (photo). Toll plaza: the Queens-side plaza was **removed**; cashless tolling since 2017-01-10, with toll gantries **just outside the Manhattan portals** [QMT]. The old plaza (blue columns, orange hanging lane markers, white island noses, "CASH" / "E-ZPass ONLY" signs) is in `exit-13-14/QMTPlaza.jpg` for an optional period look.
- Overpass: Pulaski Bridge crosses at exit 13 [QMT].
- Lighting: tall pole with a sign mount on the approach (photo). Tunnel lighting: to check.
- Beyond the edge: Manhattan skyline (Midtown supertalls) straight ahead, Long Island City glass residential towers on the right (photo). Queens ventilation building: 100 ft orange-brick Art Deco tower in the middle of Borden Ave between 2nd and 5th Sts [QMT].
- Service road: Borden Ave runs alongside [WP].
- Sources: [QMT], [NYCR], Commons [Long Island Expwy td (2022-04-13) 036](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Long_Island_Expwy_td_(2022-04-13)_036.jpg), [QMTPlaza](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:QMTPlaza.jpg). Portal shape and stonework: to check (Commons category "Queens-Midtown Tunnel, Queens portal" not read: rate-limited).
- Built (D6, 2026-11-21): the C4 Queens cashless gantry removed (confirmed: the plaza went with open-road tolling on 2017-01-10, readers at the Manhattan portals; Wikipedia [QMT] read 2026-11-21). Queens vent building added: 100 ft orange-brick Art Deco tower with a setback, over the EB tube about 280 m (baked) behind the portal, on a Borden Ave street pad at the cap level. Skyline card from D5. Portal facing, flood doors (14 x 29 ft per [QMT]) and tunnel light fittings: still to check, not modelled.
